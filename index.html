QueueSmart is an application to allow administrators to create and manage queues and for users to join queues.

"Many organizations (student service centers, clinics, advising offices, help desks) struggle with long queues and poor visibility into wait times.
Users often do not know how long they will wait, and staff have limited tools to manage demand efficiently."

  1. Authentication:
    Users and administrators use their username and password to log into the application.


  2. Managing queues:
    Real-time tracking with estimated wait times and priority adjustment.


  3. Payment:
    Administrators and users must pay to use the application.


  4. Calendar:
    Shows the administrator and user the date of their queue(s). Users also get reminders and can reschedule or cancel.


  5. Location:
    Users must be within 0.5 miles of the service location to join a queue and to check in when they arrive.


  6. Notifications:
    Users are told when their position or status changes and when it is almost their turn.


  7. Now Serving:
    A lobby screen shows who is being served and who is up next.
