Smart queue management for campus services. Join a queue, see your live position and estimated wait, and get notified when it is almost your turn.

Authentication

Users and administrators sign in with an email and password, each through their own portal.

Manage queues

Real-time tracking with estimated wait times, priorities, reordering, and serving the next person.

Notifications

Get told when your position or status changes, and when it is almost your turn.

Calendar

See the date of your queues, get reminders, and reschedule or cancel from one place.

Location check-in

Join a queue only when you are within 0.5 miles of the service, and check in when you arrive.

Payment

Administrators and users pay a small fee to create or join a queue.
